## 1. Summary

### Criterion

- **Implemented:** the port does what the original does in that area, wired into the game (not only in the engine), from the spec. Original functions replaced by modern equivalents (memory management, `_win_*`, `G*` primitives, C runtime) count as done when the visible effect is reproduced.
- **Verified against the original:** there is evidence of comparison with SimAnt running (tick vector 307/307, test bench case, capture compared pixel by pixel, pace measurement) for that part. "Seen in the code" (Ghidra pseudocode) does **not** count as verified. That is why "verified" is always ≤ "implemented".
- The percentages are the reviewer's estimates, made from the gaps listed in the specs and in the code; they are not automatic measurements. Where the area is an extension of the port (mods, tools, documentation), there is no "against the original" and the column is "n/a".

## 5. Risks and technical debt

1. ~~Duplicated tables in the JSON~~ (resolved on 2026-10-18, `comum.json`, API 0.20).
2. **Pixel adjustments measured with no known cause** ([cause unknown] in `divergencias.md`): cat 1 pixel lower in frames 0–12; the absence of the +1 with ribbon of `_win_GetObjRect` (since 2026-10-19 the ribbon text, the triangle, the levels, the ribbon numbers and the Status use a single rule, the rectangle of `_win_Recalc`, `Retangulo::do_recalc`). (9100/9101 and Status left on 2026-10-19: window 26 only aligns to the byte on creation, and the Status adjustment added to the alignment already applied.) Several of the old ones were explained on 2026-10-19 by the **driver profile** (`CS_BYTEALIGNCLIENT` rounds the client area to 8 pixels only on planar VGA: title +3, edit window, control windows, game over, Info, About, Examine, mini map) and became a driver rule in `data/resolucao.json`; what remains may be of the same nature and should be reviewed with the profile.
3. **Hypotheses in the clock.** The live Mac clock starts at the largest deadline of the save; `mac_por_tick` = 9 only holds on the test bench; without a clock, `_GameTime × 6` [hypothesis]. The cat, the dog and the boy's balloon depend on this, and with the cat and the dog **the consumption of random draws** too: a live game does not exactly reproduce one of the original.
4. **Fast/ultra pace dependent on the CPU in the original.** The port fixes 5 and 7 passes per period. `_BirdDelay` depends on `_gameCycles` and is the only non-deterministic input of the tick.
5. **Drawing that consumes the S generator outside the tick** (rain and swarm in the yard): the port does the random draws separately, between the ticks (`full_600` matches with the 17,650 steps), but the drawn positions of the rain are its own. The random draws inside the tick were resolved on 2026-10-18/19.
6. **Load reseeds S**, so captures of the original and of the port of the same state have different directions in the animations.
7. **QEMU TCG test bench** is slow and without a usable sound card; pace, clock and sound measured there may not hold on a real machine. Determinism depends on the snapshot.
8. **Evidence outside git.** Captures, `re_trabalho/` and pseudocode are not versioned (copyright); the screen percentages cannot be redone by third parties without the game and the test bench.
9. **Pace of change of the mod API** (0.20 to 0.28 in two days). Merging by key (0.26) protects data mods; scripts depend on field names that still change.
10. **Comments with `[hipótese]`, "ainda não" or "[lacuna]" in the code** (`engine/src`, `simant/src`): 55 lines in this review; no `todo!` or `unimplemented!`. They were not rechecked one by one in this round.
11. **Driver profile.** (Audio — capture in DOSBox-X and FM timbre — came in on 2026-10-19.) The default profile is `vga` (the reference test bench); whoever compares with the original in 256-color DOSBox-X should use `svga256`/`s3_dosbox`, and the old captures made there have to be read with that profile.
