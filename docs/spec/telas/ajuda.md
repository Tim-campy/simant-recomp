# Help (WinHelp) and the port's viewer

The original SimAnt opens help in Windows' WinHelp: `WinHelp(hwnd, "SIMANT.HLP", HELP_CONTEXT, n)` with the object's context number (ribbon "?" button, help mode, the windows' "?" buttons) or `HELP_INDEX` (F1). WinHelp is a separate program: it opens its own window over SimAnt, and the game keeps running. Who calls it and with which context is in the game screens (ribbon, windows, keys); here is only what WinHelp shows and how the port reproduces it. File format: [../formatos/hlp.md](../formatos/hlp.md).

Implementation: `engine/src/core/ajuda.rs` (`VisorAjuda`), data in `data/ajuda.json`. Export without a window: `cargo run -p engine --example ajuda -- <game folder> saida.png [context] [--topico N] [--rolar P] [--clicar X Y] [--listar]`.

## 1. What the original shows (QEMU bench, 2026-10-18)

Captures `re_trabalho/agente_ui/a02` (context 4, click on the edit window in help mode), `a04` (ribbon "?" button, context 0x2200), `a05` (help mode + statistics button, 0x2209) and `a06` (F1, index), script `tests/referencias/roteiros/qemu_ui_ajuda.txt`. Measurements on the original's 640 × 480 screen:

- Window "SimAnt Help - SIMANT.HLP" from x 213 to 637 and y 2 to 477, always in the same place in the four captures: thick frame of 4 (black, 2 of gray, black) with the corner marks 18 + 4 pixels from each corner.
- Title (y 6–23, blue, white text centered in the System font): 18 × 18 system menu box with the Windows long dash (13 × 3 black rectangle with a white middle at x +2, y +7), 18 × 18 minimize and maximize buttons on the right (4-line arrows), black line below (y 24).
- Menu (y 25–42, white; black line at 43): "File", "Edit", "Bookmark", "Help" in the System font, cells at x +8, +48, +89 and +170 from the inner border (217), underlined letters F, E, m, H.
- Button bar (y 44–64): dark gray background, six 59 × 21 buttons packed from the inner border (Contents, Search, Back, His**t**ory, `<<`, `>>`), black outline without the four corners, 2 lines of light and 2 of shadow, MS Sans Serif 8 pt bold text with the underlined letter, centered counting the extra bold pixel. Unused button (Back without history, `<<`/`>>` outside a browse sequence): text grayed out in a checkerboard (only the pixels with even x + y).
- Topic text (y 65–473, white): text origin 8 pixels from the inner border (x 225). Each paragraph starts on a new line; the line height is that of the largest font in it (MS Sans Serif 12 pt bold = 20, 10 pt = 16); an empty paragraph takes the height of the current font. Jumps are green (color 2), underlined 2 lines below the baseline, across the width of the text.
- The cursor stays in WinHelp and SimAnt stays behind, inactive.

## 2. The port's viewer

- **Placement:** the port has neither SimAnt's title bar nor its menu (the game area is the 640 × 442 client area, 37 lines below the top of the original's screen). The help window sits at x 213–637 as in the original and from the top to the bottom of the client area (y 0–441; `externo` in `data/ajuda.json`) [port's choice: the original goes from 2 to 477 of the screen, which would give −35 to 440 in the client area]. Because of this everything is 2 lines higher than the original relative to the frame, and the window has 34 fewer lines of text.
- **Fonts:** title, menu: System (`VGASYS.FON`, index 6 of `interface.json`); buttons: MS Sans Serif 8 pt bold (7); text: the fonts of the file's `|FONT` by size and boldness, mapped in `fontes` of `data/ajuda.json` to MS Sans Serif 10 pt (8), 12 pt (9), 10 pt bold (10), 12 pt bold (11), from the `SSERIFE.FON` of the user's Windows (WinHelp replaces the file's "Helv" with it).
- **Navigation:** clicking a jump changes the topic and saves the previous one for Back; Contents goes to the index (context 0x100); `<<` and `>>` follow the sequence in the topic header; Back goes back. Keys: Esc closes, arrows/PageUp/PageDown/Home/End scroll, Backspace = Back, a button's underlined letter triggers the button. Vertical scroll bar when the text does not fit (arrows: 16 pixels; track: one page).
- **Closing:** Esc, File → Exit, double click on the system menu box, Close from the system menu or Alt+F4 (§2.1).
- **Opening:** `VisorAjuda::abrir_contexto(n)` (HELP_CONTEXT; clears the history [hypothesis]) and `abrir_indice()` (HELP_INDEX). A context that does not exist leaves the viewer as it was.
- **Pictures:** the `bmN` (16-color DIB) are converted to the nearest color among the 16 Windows colors of the port's palette; they sit on the text line with their bottom on the baseline [hypothesis: position not compared with the original].

### 2.1 The window as a Windows window (test bench 2026-10-19)

WinHelp is a Windows 3.1 top-level window, with a thick frame, title, system menu box and minimize and maximize buttons. Scripts `tests/referencias/roteiros/qemu_ajuda_janela.txt` and `qemu_ajuda_janela2.txt` (captures `re_trabalho/ag_dlgaj/aj/h00`–`h17`, `k00`–`k08`, outside git; screen coordinates):

- **System menu** (single click on the box, h01; Alt+Space, h15): Restore (grayed), Move, Size, Minimize, Maximize, —, Close Alt+F4, —, Switch To... Ctrl+Esc. 167-pixel box with the left corner at the menu box (x 217, not at the window outline as in child windows), the top on the title line, shortcuts at column 98, underlined letters R, M, S, n, x, C, w, and a 1-pixel light gray shadow on the right and bottom starting from the second pixel. Double click on the box closes (Close).
- **Move** (h03, h04): dragging the title shows a **3-pixel** dotted XOR outline (the frame minus 1), with the dots inverted where the screen's x + y is even; the window moves on release. The window can go past the bottom edge of the screen.
- **Maximize** (h05): takes the whole screen with the frame outside, over SimAnt's title and menus; the button becomes the restore button (h06 restores to the previous place). Double click on the title maximizes and restores (h09).
- **Resize** (h07, k00–k02): by the edges and corners; the minimum is **102 × 26** (just the title with the frame). In a narrow window the menu bar wraps into lines of 18 + 1 pixels ("File Edit" / "Bookmark" / "Help") and the buttons stack one below the other (rows of 21), and the text adjusts to the width (with a horizontal scroll bar, which the port does not have).
- **Minimize** (h11): the window becomes a desktop icon, hidden behind the maximized SimAnt; SimAnt stays active. Windows' Alt+Tab (h12) shows the Program Manager; a new help request restores WinHelp in its previous place and size (h14).
- **Close and reopen** (k03): the window reopens in the place and size it had [WinHelp writes the position to `WIN.INI` on exit; hypothesis: key `M_WindowPosition`, not read].

**Port** (`simant/src/ajuda_ui.rs`, `engine/src/core/gerenciador.rs`, `data/ajuda.json` → `janela`): the WinHelp window goes into the port's own window manager, with the "desktop" equal to the port's client area; the frame (system menu box and menu, title, borders, minimize, maximize) belongs to the manager and the rest goes to the viewer. Minimum, menu, shortcut column and shadow come from `ajuda.json` → `janela`; the rest of the menu's appearance is that of the child windows (`janelas.json` → `gerenciador.menu_sistema`). Maximized, it takes the client area (it does not cover the title and the menu bar); minimized, it disappears until the next help request; Switch To... only closes the menu; the position lasts only for the session; closing from the keyboard is **Ctrl+F4**, not Alt+F4, and the system menu shows "Close Ctrl+F4" (port extension, 2026-10-19: Alt+F4 stays with the executable, which closes SimAnt with the Quit prompt) ([../../divergencias.md](../../divergencias.md)). The viewer wraps the menu bar and the button bar when the window is narrow and keeps the title on the left when it does not fit. Export: `cargo run -p engine --example ajuda -- JOGO saída.png [--externo E,T,D,B] [--maximizada] [--inativa] [--menu-sistema] [--arrasto E,T,D,B]`.

**Checked** (`tools/comparar_captura.py --mapear`, port with the viewer 2 lines higher): system menu **98.84%** in the menu region (h01; the difference is the grayed and highlighted Restore), maximized **99.90%** (h05), 102-pixel window **99.76%** (k01). Test: `janela_de_ajuda_move_redimensiona_minimiza_e_maximiza` (`simant/src/ajuda_ui.rs`).

## 4. Menus, Search, History and popups (WinHelp 3.1; test bench 2026-10-18)

Captures `re_trabalho/agente_ui/winhelp/` (`w00`–`w07` menus, `s01`–`s09` Search and History, `p01`–`p05` popup; measurements in `medidas.md`), scripts `tests/referencias/roteiros/qemu_ui_ajuda2.txt` (menus), `qemu_ui_ajuda3.txt` (Search, History) and `qemu_ui_ajuda4.txt` (Alt+S, popup). The WinHelp of the test bench's Windows 3.1 is version 3.1 (the Help menu has "Always on Top"). Coordinates are of the original's screen; in the port, everything is relative to the outer corner of the help window (x − 213, y − 2), in `data/ajuda.json` → `janelas` (optional, API 0.25). Implementation: `engine/src/core/ajuda_janelas.rs` (state, input and drawing), `formats/hlp.rs::palavras_chave` (`|KWBTREE` + `|KWDATA`).

**Menus.** Alt+F/E/M/H (or a click on the bar) opens the menu: the bar item gets a dark blue background and white text (File x 217–256, Edit 257–297, Bookmark 298–378, Help 379–424, y 25–42); the box has a 1-pixel black outline with the top on the bar's black line, a 1-pixel gray shadow on the right and bottom, 18-pixel items and 7-pixel separators (line in the middle), System text 16 pixels from the border (the "left + 17" measurement is the first pixel of the glyph). From the keyboard the first item is already highlighted. File: Open..., Print Topic, Print Setup..., Exit; Edit: Copy..., Annotate...; Bookmark: Define...; Help: How to Use Help, Always on Top, About Help.... No grayed item. In the port: arrows, Enter, the underlined letter and the click work; **Exit closes help; the other items only close the menu** (the port does not have the open, print, copy, annotate, bookmark, WINHELP.HLP or About dialogs) [divergence recorded].

**Search** (Search button, the letter S): modal "Search" dialog from (240, 48) to (638, 378): dialog frame (1 black pixel, 4 dark blue, 3 on top, white line inside), 18-pixel title with the system menu box; label "Type a word, or select one from the list. / Then choose Show Topics.", empty text box with the cursor, list of the 128 keywords (13-pixel items, 6 visible, "About" selected, 17-pixel scroll bar), 95 × 23 buttons Cancel, Show Topics (disabled until typing) and Go To (disabled), 2-pixel separator, "Select a topic, then choose Go To." and the topic list. Typing selects the first word that starts with the text and puts it at the top of the list; Show Topics (Enter, Alt+S or double click) replaces the text with the word's spelling, Cancel with Close, and lists the topics by **raw title** ("Queens (id=0x19b)"), the first one selected with the focus rectangle; Go To (Enter, Alt+G, double click) closes and opens the topic. With the dialog open, the help window has an inactive title (white, black text).

**History** (History button, the letter T): "Windows Help History" window from (213, 160) to (425, 319), with a thick frame and no minimize/maximize; list of the topics shown, **the most recent on top, with repetitions and the current one**, by raw title; the second (the previous one) is already selected. Arrows and Enter/double click go to the topic; Esc does not close it (test bench s09); double click on the system menu box closes it. Clicking outside it gives focus back to help, and it stays on screen. Up to 40 topics are kept [hypothesis].

**History position and `WIN.INI` (SVGA test bench, 2026-10-19, `qemu_svga_ajuda_guardada.txt`).** The key `[Windows Help] H_WindowPosition = [x, y, width, height, maximized]` (screen pixels) is the stored position of the **History** window, not of the main window: with the user's key (`[266,200,266,200,0]`) and with `[100,50,300,250,0]` put into the disk copy, the main window opened at the default place (W/3 to W − 3, line 2) at 640×480 and 800×600, and History opened exactly at (100, 50) with 300 × 250 (`aj_hist.png`). Without the key (VGA test bench), History sits at (213, 160) with 213 × 160 = one third of the screen in each dimension; the user's key is that same place on an 800×600 screen [hypothesis: WinHelp writes, on closing History, the place where it was]. **Port:** `data/ajuda.json` → `historico.divisor_tela` = 3 and `topo_do_visor_na_tela` = 2 (the port's viewer starts at line 0 of the client area; History moves up by the same amount); the game reads the key from the user's `WIN.INI` (the same one found for the preferences: `--win-ini` or `WINDOWS/WIN.INI` above the game; read only, even with `preferencias.json`) (`preferencias::posicao_historico_ajuda`, `VisorAjuda::definir_tela`); test `janela_history_no_lugar_do_winhelp`. In the user's installation at 640×480, History therefore opens at (266, 200) with 266 × 200.

**Popups** (0xE0 jumps): the click opens a window on top of everything with the jump's topic: 2-pixel black outline, white background, 12-pixel checkerboard shadow on the right and bottom; text in the file's fonts 9 pixels from the inner border and 1 below the top. In the only sample ("Queen Info", picture of the ant in the "Queens" topic), the window is 238 pixels wide (125–362), centered (with the shadow) on the picture and starts right below it [hypothesis: width and position from one sample]; without room below, the port moves it up as needed [hypothesis]. Any click or key closes the popup, without following the click.

**Comparison** (`cargo run -p engine --example ajuda` with `--alt`, `--tecla`, `--digitar`, `--clicar`, `--altura-popup`; `tools/comparar_captura.py --topo 2 --mapear --regiao`): File/Edit/Bookmark/Help menus 99.39 / 99.56 / 99.63 / 99.46% in the window region (the rest is the mouse cursor and the `>>`), Search open 99.68%, with "queen" 99.80%, after Show Topics 99.51% (focus rectangle and thumb off by 1 pixel), History 99.89%, popup 99.29% (the content matches pixel by pixel; the bottom of the popup goes outside the port's client area and was not compared). Test: `engine/tests/ajuda_reais.rs` (`palavras_chave_do_search`, `janelas_do_winhelp_como_na_bancada`).

## 5. Not reproduced

- The dialogs of the menu items (Open, Print Topic, Print Setup, Copy, Annotate, Define, How to Use Help, About Help) and Always on Top; the window going behind the game; the Task List (Switch To...); the horizontal scroll bar of the narrow window; maximizing over SimAnt's title and menus (§2.1).
- Highlighting a menu item with the mouse hovering over it (keyboard only); dragging in the menu.
- Exact rule for the width and position of popups (only one sample), History with a scroll bar.
